# Project Context: Integral Mass Captive Ecosystem
**Website:** captive.integralmass.com
**Mission:** To create a unified risk-management and financial suite that accelerates the manufacturing, installation, and renting of Prefabricated Homes and Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs) to address the housing crisis.

## 1. Strict Terminology & Compliance
* **NEVER USE:** "Actuarially proven," "Actuarially sound," or imply official actuarial sign-off. The model is conceptual and awaits formal actuarial review.
* **Fortuitous vs. Speculative Risk:** The platform must clearly state it insures *fortuitous risk* (unforeseen, accidental financial losses) and strictly *not speculative risk* (market gambles). All policy slips and marketing copy must reflect this distinction.

## 2. Core New Offerings to Integrate
* **Home Gap Coverage (Reference: Auto Gap):** A policy covering the delta between *expected* prefab installation costs and *actual* final costs due to fortuitous delays, site complications, or supply chain shocks.
* **GC Innovation Insurance:** Modeled after internal corporate captives. We incentivize General Contractors to specialize in prefabs by allowing them to pay premiums into the captive, ensuring against the fortuitous risks of entering a new market/product line.
* **Mortgage-as-a-Service (MaaS):** Conceptual API integration (e.g., Pyron) targeted at Student Housing and Rural Markets. This provides a unified UI for users to secure funding to buy/build prefabs. 
* **In-House Renter's Insurance Portal:** For property owners operating large prefab portfolios (e.g., trailer parks, student villages). The captive allows the owner to offer renter's insurance directly to tenants via a seamless web form/chatbot UI.

## 3. Structural Evolution
* **Beyond the 12 GCs:** The model is expanding. Renters in portfolio properties now act as independent, fortuitous risk agents.
* **Cell Captive Architecture:** The platform should introduce the concept of "Protected Cell Captives" (PCCs). This allows independent GC syndicates or large property owners to spin up their own "cells" under the Integral Mass master captive to manage their specific risk pools.
