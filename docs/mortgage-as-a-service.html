Integrated financing for ADU and prefab housing. Apply for mortgages directly on our platform through API-powered lending partners—streamlining the path from concept to construction.

Why MaaS for Student Housing?

  • Predictable Demand: University enrollment provides stable tenant pipeline
  • Quick ROI: High rental rates near campuses accelerate payback
  • Seasonal Flexibility: Summer short-term rentals boost income
  • Appreciation: Campus-adjacent properties historically outperform

Ideal Borrowers

  • Homeowners within 2 miles of ASU, U of A, NAU campuses
  • Real estate investors targeting student rental market
  • Parents building ADUs for college-age children

Why MaaS for Rural Development?

  • Underserved Market: Traditional lenders avoid rural properties
  • Lower Competition: Less saturated than urban markets
  • Community Impact: Address housing shortages in rural Arizona
  • Land Value: Large lots provide expansion potential

Ideal Borrowers

  • Rural landowners seeking to add rental income
  • Agricultural workers needing workforce housing
  • Retirees building guest houses on rural properties
